# What could have been

Running a startup was not like the books made it seem. The hard part wasn't knowing what the customer wanted, or what to build. Nor even building something people wanted.

It was sustained, strong conviction - against competitors, distractions, and the amorphous thing that is AI.

Magpai was a creative whiteboard tool, for building multi-use tools and brainstorming creative variations. Although some nodes had AI, many nodes were just easier ways to access multimedia editing operations.

Once Magpai became not that, it all became very hard.

I talked to lots of people about Magpai. But in the end, I over-biased on the wrong feedback, and had personal ambitions far too big for the shape of Magpai. Where I now see a potential acquihire or acquisition as a perfectly good option - 2024 Ben wanted to be the next Canva. The next Atlassian.

I regret pivoting. It happened gradually, and over time. And it was the biggest, hardest, most expensive lesson I have yet learnt.

\- Ben
